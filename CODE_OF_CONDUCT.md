# Code of Conduct

DepGuard welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.
Participation in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces
requires treating others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and clear when asking questions or reviewing work.
- Critique ideas and code rather than the people behind them.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect different perspectives, identities, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Good-faith security research
must follow the project's private [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation and reporting

The project maintainer, [@phcodesage](https://github.com/phcodesage), moderates
project spaces and may remove content, lock conversations, or restrict
participation to address violations. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and its impact; repeated or serious misconduct may result in a ban.

For moderation requests that can be discussed publicly, use
[Discussions](https://github.com/phcodesage/depguard/discussions) and mention
@phcodesage. Do not post personal information or sensitive incident details.

To report abuse confidentially to GitHub, use its
[Report abuse process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
This sends the report to GitHub rather than to project maintainers. GitHub's
platform rules also apply. A dedicated private maintainer conduct-reporting
channel has not yet been established.
